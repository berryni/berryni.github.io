2.0  RESEARCH

2.1  Platform trials in infectious disease

Statistical design and analysis for the PRINCIPLE and PANORAMIC COVID-19 platform trials in the UK, which tested community treatments from azithromycin and inhaled budesonide to molnupiravir and nirmatrelvir–ritonavir. Results appeared in The Lancet and the New England Journal of Medicine.

2.2  Oncology and critical care

Bayesian adaptive designs for GBM AGILE, the global phase 2/3 platform trial in glioblastoma, as well as I-SPY2, the CHIPS cold-stored platelet trial in cardiac surgery, and RESCUE-3 in ventilator weaning.1

2.3  Methods and simulation

Modelling time trends in multi-arm platform trials, using early and longitudinal endpoint data to make adaptive decisions sooner, and simulation-guided trial design. Developer of the FACTS clinical trial simulation software.
